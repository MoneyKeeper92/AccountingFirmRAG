**Event:** Accountex España 2026

## My Goals

### Primary Goal
Evaluate the current state of AI and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) implementations in Spanish tax and auditing software.

### Specific Questions to Answer

1. **Are vendors using RAG for tax law/regulatory knowledge?**
   - What knowledge sources power their AI?
   - How do they prevent hallucinations?
   - How current is their legal/regulatory database?

2. **Are vendors using AI for client data analysis?**
   - Can AI query client financial data?
   - Does it surface insights or just retrieve numbers?

3. **Is anyone combining both?**
   - Client data + regulatory knowledge in one AI workflow?
   - Advisory opportunity discovery from client data?

4. **What's production-ready vs roadmap?**
   - How many firms actually using AI features?
   - What's the real accuracy/error rate?

### Secondary Goals

- Understand the Spanish asesoría market and typical workflows
- Learn about VeriFactu compliance and how vendors are addressing it
- Network with potential partners or clients for future projects

## Red Flags to Watch For

| They Say | What It Probably Means |
|----------|------------------------|
| "Estamos trabajando en ello" | Not ready yet |
| "En la próxima versión" | Roadmap, not production |
| "Usamos ChatGPT / OpenAI" | Generic wrapper, not specialized |
| "Nuestro OCR con IA..." | Pivoting to invoice scanning |
| "Machine learning para categorización" | Rule-based, not generative AI |
| "Próximamente" | Vaporware |
