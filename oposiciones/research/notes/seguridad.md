# Official Policía Nacional and Guardia Civil Constitution-law exam materials (2010–2026)

Research date / URL-open date: **2026-11-17**. Scope is limited to first-party `interior.gob.es`, `policia.es`, `guardiacivil.es`/`web.guardiacivil.es`, and BOE-linked official material. No academy, publisher, Daypo, Quizlet, Telegram, or app questions were used or copied. Because the Policía and Guardia Civil portal notices restrict republication, this note identifies questions by number, topic, and constitutional article rather than reproducing full stems or choices.

## Which official questionnaires and answer keys are publicly available by year?

### Takeaway

The verified public record is uneven. Guardia Civil currently exposes complete questionnaires and keys for 2026 and six questionnaire models for 2022; it exposes keys but no verified questionnaires for 2020. The 2021 folder exposes results and logistics, but no questionnaire/key was found. Policía Nacional's 2025–2026 materials are announced on public pages but the actual models/keys are individualized behind “Mis Inscripciones,” so they are not public downloadable historical papers.

### Inferences

- “Published at the time” is not equivalent to “currently available as a public historical download”: the public Policía notices point to authenticated candidate areas, and Guardia Civil's current process page is overwritten with the newest year. — [Policía applicant manual](https://www.policia.es/portalaspirantes/documents/33928/0/MANUAL+DEL+ASPIRANTE.pdf/462b9bbd-f153-1028-ac5c-4d7c97c0de8a?t=1668775637663); [Guardia Civil current process page](https://web.guardiacivil.es/es/tramites/ingresos/ingresocuerpo)
- For a defensible practice-question corpus, only the verified 2022 and 2026 Guardia Civil questionnaires can presently supply official question wording; Policía materials and most other years need an access-to-information request or written authorization rather than reconstruction from unofficial copies.

### Gaps

- No official historical index covering every year 2010–2026 was found.
- Policía Nacional: no public downloadable Escala Básica questionnaire was verified for any year; 2025–2026 notices are public but the files are authenticated.
- Guardia Civil: questionnaires/keys remain unverified for 2010–2019, 2021, and 2023–2025; questionnaire wording is unverified for 2020.
- The 2022 knowledge-answer templates may exist under filenames not indexed by search; only questionnaire models and aptitude templates were verified.

## Which questions test the Spanish Constitution or constitutional institutions?

### Takeaway

Verified Constitution blocks occur in all inspected 2022 Guardia Civil models and in both 2026 models. Model B generally reorders the same question set. The inventory below records numbers, topics, and the controlling constitutional provisions without reproducing full questions.

## Are official historical papers complete or missing?

### Takeaway

They are substantially incomplete as a public historical archive. The strongest complete sets are Guardia Civil 2022 and 2026; Policía's recent papers are candidate-gated, and many Guardia years retain only process/results documents or keys.

### Cited Findings

- Policía explicitly routes exam-template downloads through the applicant's own registration detail page, which explains why public news notices do not provide reusable historical files. — [Official applicant manual](https://www.policia.es/portalaspirantes/documents/33928/0/MANUAL+DEL+ASPIRANTE.pdf/462b9bbd-f153-1028-ac5c-4d7c97c0de8a?t=1668775637663)
- Guardia Civil's 2026 process page exposes current keys and describes the correction history, while older years are not linked from that page. — [Official 2026 process page](https://web.guardiacivil.es/es/tramites/ingresos/ingresocuerpo)
- The 2020 official archive evidence consists of keys for four exercises, not question booklets. — [24 October 2020 key](https://web.guardiacivil.es/export/sites/guardiaCivil/documentos/pdfs/2020/IngresoGC2020/PLANTILLA_INGRESO_GC_24OCT20.pdf); [25 October 2020 key](https://web.guardiacivil.es/export/sites/guardiaCivil/documentos/pdfs/2020/IngresoGC2020/PLANTILLA_INGRESO_GC_25OCT20.pdf)
- The 2021 official folder evidence consists of results, sedes, and temario updates; no question booklet/key was found. — [2021 results](https://web.guardiacivil.es/export/sites/guardiaCivil/documentos/pdfs/2021/ingresoGC2021/08-RESOLUCION-DEFINITIVA-RESULTADOS-CONOCIMIENTOS-INGRESO-2021.pdf); [2021 sedes](https://web.guardiacivil.es/export/sites/guardiaCivil/documentos/pdfs/2021/ingresoGC2021/INFORMACIxN_SEDES_EXAMEN_Y_DISTRIBUCION_AULAS_EXAMENES_INGRESO_2021.pdf)

### Inferences

- Absence from current search/indexing does not prove a document was never published; it proves only that no currently openable first-party URL was verified on 2026-11-17.
- A complete 2010–2026 corpus should therefore label each year as “verified questionnaire,” “verified key only,” “authenticated/non-public,” or “not located,” rather than silently filling gaps from academy rehosts.

### Gaps

- Historical URLs may have been removed, renamed, de-indexed, or retained only in authenticated areas.
- BOE convocations verify that a selection process existed but generally do not contain the administered questionnaire or answer key.
- Third-party rehosts may preserve missing files, but under the research constraint they were not copied or used to identify question wording.

## What reuse/legal terms govern Interior, policia.es, and guardiacivil.es?

### Takeaway

The sites do not share one uniform rule. Interior's legal notice broadly authorizes commercial and non-commercial reuse with attribution and integrity conditions. Policía's applicant portal permits download/private use only absent express authorization. Guardia Civil likewise limits ordinary content to private use, with a separate exception only for documents explicitly published as reusable. Therefore, full exam text should not be republished from Policía or Guardia Civil without written authorization or an explicit reusable designation.

### Cited Findings

- **Ministerio del Interior:** its legal notice authorizes copying, dissemination, modification, adaptation, extraction, rearrangement, and combination for commercial or non-commercial reuse, with a free non-exclusive IP licence. Conditions include not distorting meaning, citing “Origen de los datos: Ministerio del Interior,” retaining the last-update date and metadata, and not implying Ministry sponsorship. Opened successfully 2026-11-17. — [Interior legal notice](https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/gl/aviso-legal/)
- **Policía Nacional / Portal del Aspirante:** reproduction, distribution, public communication, transformation, or similar activity is prohibited absent express authorization; the granted licence is limited to downloading and private use while keeping content intact. Opened successfully 2026-11-17. — [Policía applicant-portal legal notice](https://www.policia.es/portalaspirantes/aviso-legal)
- **Guardia Civil:** ordinary portal content may be downloaded for private use only; reproduction, distribution, public communication, or similar activity requires express authorization at `sugerencias@guardiacivil.org`. An exception exists for documents explicitly published as reusable; those must retain meaning, cite source, mention the last publication date, and preserve reuse/update metadata. Opened successfully 2026-11-17. — [Guardia Civil legal notice](https://web.guardiacivil.es/es/informacion-general/aviso-legal/)

### Inferences

- Hosting on an official government domain does not itself establish that a Policía or Guardia Civil exam PDF is a “reusable document.”
- Safe use for this project is bibliographic inventory, question-number/topic/article indexing, links, and short factual descriptions. Full stems/choices require either (a) an explicit reusable designation, or (b) written permission from the relevant directorate.
- Interior's broad notice should not be assumed to override the more specific terms displayed on `policia.es` or `guardiacivil.es`.

### Gaps

- None of the examined Guardia Civil questionnaire PDFs displayed a clear per-document reusable-data label in the extracted text.
- No express written authorization from Policía or Guardia Civil was obtained during this research.
- Legal notices can change; terms should be re-opened and archived on the date any publication corpus is released.
