# Official Seguridad Social and AEAT exam papers: Constitution-law inventory (2010–2026)

## Which official questionnaires and keys exist per year/model/access route?

### Takeaway
The strongest official run is AEAT Agentes de la Hacienda Pública: official process pages verify questionnaires and provisional/final keys for 2020–2026 (using exam year, which is often one year after the OEP label). Seguridad Social has a complete, clearly indexed C1 Administrativo set for the OEP-2023 process examined in 2024, plus extraordinary papers in 2025; earlier Seguridad Social C1 material is fragmented. No distinct official “Auxiliar Administrativo de la Seguridad Social” exam series was verified.

### Inferences
- OEP year and sitting year must be stored separately. For example, “OEP 2025” is the process whose first exercise occurred in 2026; labeling files only by page-folder year will misdate practice questions.
- “Acceso B” and “Acceso R” on older AEAT promoción interna pages are official route/model labels and should not be silently normalized to ordinary A/B models.

### Gaps
- **AEAT 2019:** targeted official-domain search did not locate an Agentes de la Hacienda Pública process page; the only 2019 result was for Cuerpo Técnico de Hacienda, so 2019 remains missing rather than “no exam.”
- **AEAT 2010–2018:** no complete official inventory was verified in the available research window. Commercial and UGC mirrors were deliberately excluded.
- **Seguridad Social 2010–2020:** no complete C1 Administrativo official paper/key sets were verified.
- **Separate Auxiliar Administrativo Seguridad Social:** no official selection series under that exact title was found. Search hits for “auxiliar administrativo” were occupational/cotización categories, not exam papers; those are excluded.
- Direct PDF URLs for every AEAT 2023–2026 questionnaire/key were not individually expanded from the official HTML pages. The process pages themselves were opened and verify existence, route, model, and provisional/final status.

## What is missing, what is only rehosted, and what reuse terms apply?

### Takeaway
Both official sites broadly permit commercial and non-commercial reuse, including reproduction and modification, subject to source attribution, no distortion, and update-date requirements. AEAT additionally states no implied endorsement and preservation of reuse/update metadata. Official question text can therefore be reused unless a document carries an express contrary notice, but this note intentionally records only topics and article numbers.

### Cited Findings
- Seguridad Social authorizes total or partial reproduction, modification, distribution, and communication for commercial and non-commercial uses, unless expressly stated otherwise; users must not distort the information, must cite the source, and must mention the last-update date. Opened successfully on 2026-11-17. — [Seguridad Social legal notice](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/AvisoLegal)
- AEAT uses the basic reuse regime under art. 8.1 of Real Decreto 1495/2011; its conditions prohibit distortion, require citation of the public-sector source, require the last-update date when present, prohibit suggesting sponsorship/endorsement, and require preservation of update/reuse metadata. Opened successfully on 2026-11-17. — [AEAT reuse conditions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/gobierno-abierto/reutilizacion-informacion/condiciones-reutilizacion.html)
- AEAT’s legal-use page separately confirms that, absent an express contrary indication, information on its site may be reproduced wholly or partly, modified, distributed, and communicated for commercial and non-commercial use. Opened successfully on 2026-11-17. — [AEAT use of web information](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/condiciones-uso-sede-electronica/aviso-legal/utilizacion-informacion-contenida-web-aeat.html)

### Inferences
- Before reproducing any full official question, retain the source URL, document update/publication date where present, official body name, model/access route, and any embedded metadata or special notice.
- A third-party mirror should be classified as “rehosted only” unless the same file or metadata can be tied back to an official seg-social.es, sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es, agenciatributaria.gob.es, or BOE URL.

### Gaps
- No commercial/UGC banks were consulted, so no mirror-by-mirror provenance table was created.
- Several older papers may survive only in search indexes or third-party mirrors; without an opened official original they remain unverified and should not supply question text.
- The Seguridad Social legal-notice fetch rendered in English despite the Spanish portal URL; the substantive permission and conditions were readable, but a Spanish-language snapshot should be retained before publication if exact Spanish wording is needed.
