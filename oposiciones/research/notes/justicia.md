# Official Spanish Justicia exam papers: Constitution-law inventory (2010–2026)

## Which official questionnaires and definitive/provisional keys exist per body/year/model?

### Takeaway
The Ministry archive is organized by convocatoria order rather than a reliable annual index. The verified core covers the 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2024 and 2025 convocatorias (exams held in 2016–2026); older 2010 material is not safely attributable from the surviving generic filename alone. “Year” below therefore distinguishes the convocatoria year from the exam date.

## Which questions test the Constitución Española or constitutional institutions?

### Takeaway
Question numbers can be verified only where the official questionnaire itself was retrieved. The strongest verified mappings are 2018 Gestión, 2024 incidencias, and 2026 Auxilio. Topics include the Crown, Cortes, Government, constitutional rights, territorial organization, Tribunal Constitucional, CGPJ and Defensor del Pueblo.

### Cited Findings
- **Auxilio Judicial, PJC/1549/2025, 2026, first exercise Model A:** Q29 (CE art. 82, legislative delegation by Cortes to Government); Q30 (CE art. 17 and organic-law regulation of habeas corpus); Q31 (Defensor del Pueblo and Title I); Q41 (confidence question); Q42 (failed investiture, dissolution and the King); Q44 (motion of censure); Q46 (CE art. 141, province); Q49 (CE art. 152, TSJ in autonomous-community judicial organization). Model B contains the same topics in reordered positions Q79, Q80, Q81, Q91, Q92, Q94, Q96 and Q99. — [Official Model A](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/Ciudadano/EmpleoPublico/Documents/Primer%20ejercicio%20-%20Modelo%20A%20-%20AUXJUD.pdf); [Official Model B](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/Ciudadano/EmpleoPublico/Documents/Primer%20ejercicio%20-%20Modelo%20B%20-%20AUXJUD.pdf), opened **2026-11-17**.
- **Auxilio 2026 broader constitutional-institution coverage:** Model A Q22 concerns criminal jurisdiction over the Defensor del Pueblo; Q26 asks which institution may extend mercantile-jurisdiction territory; Q57–58 concern CGPJ organization. These are constitutional-institution questions but are framed primarily through the LOPJ rather than a direct CE article. — [Official Model A](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/Ciudadano/EmpleoPublico/Documents/Primer%20ejercicio%20-%20Modelo%20A%20-%20AUXJUD.pdf), opened **2026-11-17**.

### Inferences
- A useful tagging scheme is: **direct CE article**, **constitutional organ/institution**, **constitutional procedure**, and **LOPJ implementation of a constitutional institution**. This avoids treating every mention of “Gobierno” or “Cortes” as a Constitution question.
- Model A/B numbering frequently changes while substance remains identical; mappings should be recorded per model rather than assigning one number to the exam generally.

### Gaps
- Constitution-topic question numbers were not assigned where only an answer key, not the official questionnaire, was verified.
- The retrieved 2018, 2024 and 2026 mappings are not an exhaustive reading of every official questionnaire in the inventory.
- No full question text is reproduced because at least one official questionnaire contains an explicit no-reproduction notice.

### Gaps

- No checksum or local preservation copy was created; future URL reuse could change what a generic link serves.

## What reuse terms govern Ministerio de Justicia documents?

### Takeaway
The general portal notice limits ordinary portal-content use to download/private use unless expressly authorized, while the Ministry’s open-data page authorizes commercial and non-commercial reuse for documents submitted to those conditions. Individual exam questionnaires may impose a stricter express no-reproduction notice, so metadata extraction is safe for this project but verbatim question reproduction should not proceed without document-specific clearance.

### Cited Findings
- The Ministry’s general [Aviso Legal](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/aviso-legal) states that portal design, code, logos, marks, images and content are protected; use, reproduction, distribution, public communication or transformation is prohibited absent express authorization, and the user license is limited to download and private use with content kept intact. It directs users to the open-data section for reusable information. Opened **2026-11-17**.
- The Ministry’s [open-data reuse terms](https://sede.mjusticia.gob.es/informacion-ayuda/datos-abiertos-ministerio) authorize reuse of documents subject to those terms for commercial and non-commercial purposes, including copying, dissemination, modification, adaptation, extraction, rearrangement and combination, with a free non-exclusive IP license. Opened **2026-11-17**.
- Those open-data conditions require: do not distort meaning; cite **“Origen de los datos: Ministerio de Justicia”**; state the last-update date when present; do not imply Ministry participation, sponsorship or endorsement; and preserve applicable update/reuse metadata. — [Official open-data terms](https://sede.mjusticia.gob.es/informacion-ayuda/datos-abiertos-ministerio), opened **2026-11-17**.
- The official 2024 Gestión incidencias questionnaire adds a document-specific term: “No se permite la reproducción total o parcial de este cuestionario incluso citando la procedencia.” — [Official questionnaire](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/Ciudadano/EmpleoPublico/Documents/Examen%20Primer%20Ejercicio%20-%20Incidencias.pdf), opened **2026-11-17**.

### Inferences
- Do **not** assume every employment PDF is automatically a dataset “submitted” to the open-data conditions merely because it is hosted by the Ministry.
- For practice-question development, retain only bibliographic metadata, question number, topic, cited article and answer-key status unless the specific document clearly carries an affirmative reuse license.
- If full official wording is desired, obtain written permission or a Ministry clarification covering exam questionnaires, especially those carrying an express no-reproduction legend.

### Gaps
- The relationship between the general open-data authorization and the questionnaire-specific prohibition is not resolved by the pages themselves.
- No document-specific license metadata was found embedded in the retrieved exam PDFs beyond the explicit no-reproduction notice in the 2024 Gestión incidencias questionnaire.
