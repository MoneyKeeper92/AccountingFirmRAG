# Reuse, datasets, market, and copyright treatment of Spanish-oposiciones Constitution questions

Research date / `opened_on` for all sources below: **2026-11-17**. This is source research, not a legal conclusion or legal advice. “Lower-risk” below means that the publisher has given an express reuse permission covering the described material; it does not decide whether a question is independently excluded from copyright by article 13 LPI.

## Exact reuse terms for official question text

### Takeaway

The clearest ingestion candidates are question papers expressly placed in an open-data catalogue—especially the Generalitat de Catalunya examination dataset—and INAP documents to which INAP’s open-data notice applies. The legal notices are not uniform: Interior, Seguridad Social and AEAT are broadly permissive; Policía Nacional and Justice default to private use only; Guardia Civil permits reuse only for documents affirmatively designated “reutilizables.” Tesón should therefore preserve source-level licence evidence rather than infer permission merely from an official URL.

### Cited Findings

- **Baseline law is reuse, not a declaration that every public document is public domain.** Ley 37/2007 defines reuse as use of documents for commercial or non-commercial purposes, but excludes, among other things, documents restricted under access laws, third-party-IP documents, logos/insignia, some educational/research/cultural holdings, confidential material and protected personal data. Its exact third-party clause is: “Los documentos sobre los que existan derechos de propiedad intelectual o industrial por parte de terceros”; it then says public bodies’ own IP should be exercised “de forma que se facilite su reutilización.” — [BOE, consolidated Ley 37/2007](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-19814) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **RD 1495/2011 makes broad reuse the state-sector default, but only for documents within scope.** Article 2 states that state bodies “autorizarán la reutilización de los documentos elaborados o custodiados por ellos e incluidos en el ámbito de aplicación”; the preamble describes article 8.1 as reuse “sin sujeción a condiciones específicas,” subject to the general conditions, and says third-party-IP material may be authorised only with sufficient rights. — [BOE, consolidated RD 1495/2011](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2011-17560) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **The reusable-document baseline conditions are attribution and integrity controls, not a non-commercial restriction.** datos.gob.es says: “permiten la reutilización… para fines comerciales y no comerciales” and expressly includes “copia, difusión, modificación, adaptación, extracción, reordenación y combinación.” Its conditions are: do not distort meaning; cite source; mention last-update date when supplied; do not imply sponsorship; preserve update/licence metadata. — [datos.gob.es legal notice](https://datos.gob.es/es/avisolegal) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)

#### State bodies

- **INAP — permissive for documents subjected to its open-data conditions; commercial use and modification are express.** Exact grant: “permiten la reutilización de los documentos sometidos a ellas para fines comerciales y no comerciales” and “incluye… la copia, difusión, modificación, adaptación, extracción, reordenación y combinación.” Required attribution is “Origen de los datos: Instituto Nacional de Administración Pública”; the update date and licence metadata must be retained, meaning must not be distorted, and endorsement must not be implied. The same page separately says portal content cannot be reproduced without citing origin or seeking authorisation, so Tesón should retain proof that each exam file is a document covered by the open-data/reuse portion, not merely any INAP webpage. — [INAP legal notice](https://www.inap.es/es/aviso-legal) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Justice Ministry — default is restrictive; use only an item specifically in its open-data section or with permission.** Exact clause: “Su uso, reproducción, distribución, comunicación pública, transformación o cualquier otra actividad similar o análoga, queda totalmente prohibida salvo que medie expresa autorización”; the portal licence is limited to “la descarga… y el uso privado.” The notice directs users to the Ministry’s open-data section for separately reusable information. A publicly accessible Justice exam PDF is therefore not enough by itself to establish commercial reuse. — [Ministerio de Justicia legal notice](https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/aviso-legal) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Interior Ministry — broad commercial and non-commercial reuse.** Its exact grant includes “copia, difusión, modificación, adaptación, extracción, reordenación y combinación” and a free, non-exclusive IP licence for reproduction, distribution, public communication and transformation. Conditions require “Origen de los datos: Ministerio del Interior,” last-update date, no distortion, no implied support and preserved metadata. — [Ministerio del Interior legal notice](https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/gl/aviso-legal/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Policía Nacional Portal del Aspirante — restrictive, despite being inside Interior.** Exact clause: “Su uso, reproducción, distribución, comunicación pública, transformación o cualquier otra actividad similar o análoga, queda totalmente prohibida salvo que medie expresa autorización de la Dirección General de la Policía”; permitted use is only download and private use while contents remain intact. Treat Police question papers as **metadata/link-only unless a specific file has a separate reuse statement or written authorisation**. — [Policía Portal del Aspirante legal notice](https://www.policia.es/portalaspirantes/aviso-legal) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Guardia Civil — designation-based, not a portal-wide open grant.** Exact clause: “La licencia de uso de cualquier contenido de este Portal se limita… al uso privado… Excepto los documentos reutilizables publicados en determinadas secciones.” For designated reusable documents: “No desnaturalizar,” “Citar la fuente,” “Mencionar la fecha de la última publicación,” and preserve update/licence metadata. Otherwise reproduction/distribution/public communication requires express authorisation. Tesón should not infer that an exam is reusable unless the section/file marks it so. — [Guardia Civil legal notice](https://web.guardiacivil.es/es/informacion-general/aviso-legal/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Seguridad Social — broad express grant.** It authorises commercial and non-commercial reuse, including copy, dissemination, modification, adaptation, extraction, rearrangement and combination, with a free non-exclusive IP licence. Attribution formula: “Origen de los datos: Seguridad Social”; also preserve update date and metadata, do not distort meaning or imply support. — [Seguridad Social reuse notice](https://www.seg-social.es/wps/portal/wss/internet/InformacionUtil/596/?changeLanguage=es) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **AEAT — broad “unless otherwise stated” grant.** Exact clause: “salvo indicación expresa en contrario… autorizada su reproducción total o parcial, modificación, distribución y comunicación, para usos comerciales y no comerciales”; conditions are no distortion, cite source and mention the latest update when available. AEAT’s reuse page also identifies the basic modality under article 8.1 RD 1495/2011 and adds no implied sponsorship and metadata preservation. — [AEAT web-use notice](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/condiciones-uso-sede-electronica/aviso-legal/utilizacion-informacion-contenida-web-aeat.html) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [AEAT reuse conditions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/gobierno-abierto/reutilizacion-informacion/condiciones-reutilizacion.html) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)

#### Selected autonomous communities and cities

- **Generalitat de Catalunya — strongest question-specific source found.** datos.gob.es lists a public dataset specifically titled “Enunciados de los exámenes…” covering exam statements and correction keys, with CSV/JSON/XML access and a licence link to the Generalitat’s open-information licence. — [datos.gob.es catalogue record](https://datos.gob.es/es/catalogo/a09002970-enunciados-de-los-examenes-de-las-convocatorias-para-el-acceso-a-la-funcion-publica-de-la-generalitat-de-catalunya) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- The Catalan licence permits “la reutilización,” “la distribución y la comunicación pública,” and “la transformación… para todo el mundo y sin ninguna limitación temporal,” unless a dataset-specific notice prevails. Conditions include no alteration/distortion, attribution as “Generalitat de Catalunya. Departamento de [nombre]…,” latest-update date, personal-data safeguards, no sublicensing, and no logos/marks. It does not impose a general non-commercial limitation; specific licences may define commercial or non-commercial uses. — [Generalitat open-information licence](https://web.gencat.cat/es/generalitat/dades-indicadors/dades-obertes/llicencies) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Junta de Andalucía — CC BY 3.0 for portal texts, with an express commercial grant.** Exact clauses: “Copiar, redistribuir y comunicar públicamente,” “Hacer un uso comercial,” and “Generar obras derivadas.” Required visible credit is “Información obtenida del Portal de la Junta de Andalucía”; derivatives must carry the same attribution obligation, meaning must not be distorted, and endorsement/logos must not be implied/used. This is useful only where the Junta owns the text and no item-specific exception applies. — [Junta de Andalucía legal notice](https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/informacion/legal.html) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Comunidad de Madrid — broad reuse but an express IP exclusion.** Exact clause: it permits reuse of all site data/content unless stated otherwise, provided content and meaning are not altered, source and extraction/use date are stated, and no support is implied. It excludes content subject to intellectual/industrial property, personal-data protection or other protected rights/interests. — [Comunidad de Madrid legal notice](https://www.comunidad.madrid/atencion-ciudadano/aviso-legal-privacidad) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Ayuntamiento de València — broad reuse unless reserved, but protected works follow their own licence.** Conditions: “No alterar,” “No desnaturalizar,” “Mencionar la fuente,” and no implied municipal support. It expressly says reuse of protected works is subject to the applicable IP licence and is prohibited where rights are reserved. — [València legal notice](https://www.valencia.es/cas/aviso-legal) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Ayuntamiento de Zaragoza — expressly commercial and modifiable.** Exact grant covers commercial/non-commercial “copia, difusión, modificación, adaptación, extracción, reordenación y combinación”; required credit is “Origen de los datos: Ayuntamiento de Zaragoza (o… organismo…)”, with update date and licence metadata preserved, no distortion and no implied support. — [Zaragoza legal notice](https://zaragoza.es/sede/portal/aviso-legal) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)

### Inferences

- **Operational allowlist:** ingest verbatim only where Tesón stores (1) original official URL, (2) publisher, exam/convocation and retrieval date, (3) exact licence URL and a snapshot/hash of the notice, (4) required attribution/update metadata, and (5) confirmation that no item-specific notice overrides the portal rule. The Generalitat exam dataset is the best first source; designated INAP open-data exam documents are next.
- **Operational deny/review list:** Police, generic Justice pages, and undesignated Guardia Civil documents should remain URL/title/year/body/convocation metadata only. The same applies where a PDF says “prohibida la reproducción,” includes third-party questions, or the portal-level terms conflict with the file.
- **Do not collapse parent and sub-agency terms.** Interior’s broad notice does not safely override the more specific restrictive Policía Portal del Aspirante notice.
- **Generate-from-law is distinct from copying exams.** BOE statutory text falls within article 13 LPI’s express exclusions, but Tesón’s independently authored questions/explanations can themselves be protected. That route avoids reliance on ambiguous exam-paper status.

### Gaps

- No question-by-question inventory was made of every INAP, Justice, Police, Guardia Civil, Seguridad Social or AEAT exam PDF. Each file still needs an item-level notice check.
- No official reuse notice specific to Tribunal Constitucional-law exam questions was found for every CCAA/city. The selected examples above should not be generalised to other administrations.
- Portal terms can change; storing the `opened_on` date alone is weaker than retaining a contemporaneous PDF/WARC or signed licence record.

## Openly licensed datasets and provenance

### Takeaway

Only two clearly useful open resources were found: the official Generalitat exam-enunciados dataset under the Catalan open-information licence, and ESQAD under CC BY 4.0 (useful for legal-question generation research, but not a Constitution-oposiciones bank). Several apparently “open” Hugging Face/GitHub corpora have missing, mismatched or uploader-only licences and should not be commercially scraped without verifying rights at the original source.

### Cited Findings

- **Official Generalitat exam dataset — affirmative open-data provenance.** It identifies the Generalitat as publisher, describes exam statements and correction templates, exposes machine-readable distributions, and links the Catalan reuse licence. This is the cleanest discovered source for official question text, subject to dataset-specific notices and the licence conditions above. — [datos.gob.es catalogue](https://datos.gob.es/es/catalogo/a09002970-enunciados-de-los-examenes-de-las-convocatorias-para-el-acceso-a-la-funcion-publica-de-la-generalitat-de-catalunya) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [Catalan licence](https://web.gencat.cat/es/generalitat/dades-indicadors/dades-obertes/llicencies) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **ESQAD (Zenodo) — explicit CC BY 4.0 dataset / MIT code.** It includes Spanish educational QA, 923 validated generated pairs, and legal FAQs generated/extracted from Leyes 39/2015 and 40/2015. The record says exactly: “Datasets: CC BY 4.0 - Source Code: MIT License.” Commercial reuse is permitted by CC BY with attribution, but it is not a Constitution-oposiciones question bank and provenance of each input subset should still be checked before verbatim reuse. — [Zenodo ESQAD record](https://zenodo.org/records/16351733) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Hugging Face `aya-global-exams-spanish` — relevant but licence metadata is not sufficient by itself.** The 466-row Spanish split contains INAP-origin questions and records a per-row value “AGPL …, GPL,” while linking the INAP selection portal. AGPL/GPL are software licences and the card does not establish that the uploader can relicense every underlying exam text; use the original INAP file and INAP terms instead of relying on this label. — [Hugging Face dataset card/viewer](https://huggingface.co/datasets/amayuelas/aya-global-exams-spanish) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Hugging Face `SASLeaderboard/sas_opposition_exam_data` — official-source claim, no stated licence.** Its card says the questions come from Servicio Andaluz de Salud exams and links the official source, but the repository search exposed no licence. Public availability and official provenance do not by themselves grant commercial scraping rights. — [Hugging Face SAS dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SASLeaderboard/sas_opposition_exam_data) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Hugging Face `SpaLawEx` — no reliable licence grant found.** The card says it extracted 2022–2023 Spanish bar-access exam questions; no licence statement appeared in the retrieved card. Do not ingest commercially without an original-source licence/permission analysis. — [Hugging Face SpaLawEx](https://huggingface.co/datasets/LenguajeNaturalAI/SpaLawEx) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Hugging Face `spanish-oposiciones-corpus` — high-risk provenance despite a public card.** It is gated and says it scraped Wayback snapshots of INAP plus commercial sites `oposinet.com`, `preparadores.es`, and `opositatest.com`; its own “License” field says “Public archive snapshots. Verify rights before commercial use.” Archival availability is not a rights grant, and the mix contains commercial third-party material. — [Hugging Face corpus card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Pepere45/spanish-oposiciones-corpus) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **GitHub `OpoRuta/examenes-inap-auxiliar-administrativo` — useful index/transform, but uploader’s licence cannot settle source rights.** The repo advertises about 290 official INAP questions from 2018/2019/2022/2024, labels its JSON/repository MIT, and asserts the exams are public domain. MIT can cover the uploader’s code/selection/structure, but the “public domain” assertion is not an official INAP determination; verify every source against INAP’s notice. — [GitHub repository](https://github.com/OpoRuta/examenes-inap-auxiliar-administrativo) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **GitHub `picuino/test` — real CC BY-SA 4.0, but not a discovered Constitution bank.** The repository is a general YAML multiple-choice bank/tooling project under CC BY-SA 4.0. Commercial reuse is possible under attribution/share-alike, but individual file headers/credits must be checked and topical relevance is limited. — [GitHub picuino/test](https://github.com/picuino/test/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **GitHub `MGS2025/etrivium-iam-tema-1` — relevant content but no safe grant found.** Its public metadata says 150 Constitution questions, with questions 1–90 “aportado por el cliente” and 91–150 newly drafted, and marks the material “Pendiente validación.” No licence was shown in the retrieved record; client-origin provenance is an additional red flag. Keep as metadata only. — [GitHub file](https://github.com/MGS2025/etrivium-iam-tema-1/blob/main/tema-1-test.md) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Kaggle — no relevant Spanish Constitution-oposiciones dataset found in the search.** Results were parliamentary speeches, Indian Constitution data, general trivia and non-Spanish exams, not a licensable Spanish-oposiciones question corpus. — [Kaggle search result: Spanish Congress speeches](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cabreraalex/spanish-congress-of-deputies-speeches) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Zenodo/academic repositories — no Constitution-oposiciones bank found beyond adjacent resources.** ESQAD is the closest open QA resource; other results concerned Constitutional Court rulings or parliamentary records rather than exam questions. — [Zenodo ESQAD](https://zenodo.org/records/16351733) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [Zenodo Constitutional Court rulings dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/17295357) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)

### Inferences

- A repository licence often covers **code, schema or the uploader’s compilation**, not necessarily copied source questions. Commercial ingestion should require a provenance chain ending in an official reuse permission or a licence from the actual author/rightsholder.
- “Official source,” “public,” “open,” “archived,” and “MIT repository” are not interchangeable. Tesón should model separate fields for `source_rights`, `dataset_compilation_license`, `code_license`, and `provenance_confidence`.
- Recommended commercial-ingestion tiers:
  1. **Green:** official machine-readable exam dataset with an express commercial/open licence and no contrary item notice.
  2. **Amber:** third-party transform of green source; re-download from the official source and reproduce the transform internally.
  3. **Red:** no licence, Wayback scrape, mixed commercial/UGC origins, gated terms, or uploader claims broader rights than the original publisher.

### Gaps

- Hugging Face and GitHub searches are not exhaustive; repository cards can change and licence files may differ by revision.
- No usable Kaggle Constitution-oposiciones dataset was found, and no Spanish academic repository yielded a directly relevant open bank.
- Dataset cards rarely identify whether distractors, answer keys and explanations were authored by the tribunal, a contractor or the uploader; that missing chain of title blocks confident commercial scraping.

## Public market metadata for third-party banks

### Takeaway

The market ranges from small single-law subscriptions to broad guided courses, with public claims from roughly 730 to 13,000+ Constitution questions. Tesón can safely map provider, URL, claimed count, pricing, access model, update date, free-demo availability, explanations and feature set without storing or reproducing the questions themselves.

### Cited Findings

- **OpositaTest:** public Constitution product prices were €15.99/1 month, €71.94/6 months and €95.88/12 months; the platform says registration is free and subscribers receive unlimited tests and updated questions. — [1-month product](https://www.opositatest.com/productos/preguntas-sobre-constitucion) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [6-month product](https://www.opositatest.com/productos/preguntas-sobre-constitucion-6) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [12-month product](https://www.opositatest.com/productos/preguntas-sobre-constitucion-12) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **GoKoan:** its Constitution course publicly claimed 1,381 test questions, 978 memorisation activities, 56 diagrams, 132 audio items and 8 PDF topics, updated 2026-02-20. General Método pricing was €135/3 months, €240/6 months and €300/12 months; its broader plan claimed 30,267 total questions across offerings. — [GoKoan Constitution course](https://www.gokoan.com/normativa-general/la-constitucion-espa%C3%B1ola-de-1978) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [GoKoan prices](https://www.gokoan.com/precio) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Adams:** its Constitution product publicly claimed 3,253 questions, justified answers, progress comparison and no automatic renewal; the retrieved page displayed “Desde 19,99 €” and also a €54.99 single-payment access option, so price should be stored as plan-dependent rather than one canonical figure. — [Adams product page](https://www.adams.es/producto/adams-test/oposiciones/test-constitucion-espanola-50357/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **TestJusticia:** public claim of 3,500+ jurist-created/reviewed questions, weekly updates, statistics and explanations; listed plans included €3/1 month, €6/3 months and €12/year, plus a one-month demo with 500 questions. — [TestJusticia Constitution page](https://www.testjusticia.es/test-constitucion-espanola-de-1978/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **PreparaOposiciones:** advertised a Constitution bank of 13,300+/13,900+ questions (the page was internally inconsistent), article-linked explanations, a first free test, and “Solo Tests” from €6.90/month; it also displayed a €39 three-month pack. Store both the claim and observation date, not a normalised exact count. — [PreparaOposiciones Constitution course](https://www.preparaoposiciones.com/blog/cursos/constitucion/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **InnoTest:** advertised 3,000+ questions in the main copy and 4,000+ in its FAQ, with explanations, error/unanswered modes, audio and article diagrams; public free and Premium tiers were described but no price appeared in the retrieved page. — [InnoTest Constitution](https://innotest.es/test-constitucion-espanola-2026/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Free banks:** Red Opositor claimed 730+ questions, seven practice modes, no registration and detailed explanations; ExamenOposicion claimed 5,000+ free questions; TestMiOpo claimed 1,125/1,146 own questions and eight free no-registration simulations. These are useful competitive metadata, not content sources. — [Red Opositor free test](https://redopositor.com/test-constitucion-espanola/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [ExamenOposicion](https://examenoposicion.com/test-constitucion-espanola/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [TestMiOpo](https://testmiopo.com/test-gratis/constitucion-espanola/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **UGC/open-repository comparators:** the MGS2025 GitHub project publicly describes 150 Constitution questions but has uncertain client provenance and no visible licence; Quizlet and similar UGC services should likewise be indexed only at provider/page/topic/count level unless a creator-specific licence is verified. — [MGS2025 GitHub file](https://github.com/MGS2025/etrivium-iam-tema-1/blob/main/tema-1-test.md) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)

### Inferences

- Suggested metadata schema: `provider`, `product_name`, `url`, `free_or_paid`, `price_observed`, `billing_period`, `claimed_question_count`, `official_vs_original_claim`, `explanations`, `article_citations`, `practice_modes`, `registration_required`, `last_updated_claim`, `opened_on`, and `evidence_url`.
- Counts are marketing claims and may mix questions, tests, simulations or platform-wide totals. Preserve the exact claim and date rather than comparing them as audited inventory.
- Do not crawl demo sessions or authenticated question endpoints. Public product pages provide enough data for market positioning without reproducing commercial/UGC expression.

### Gaps

- Some providers hide final prices, duration and exact counts behind registration or checkout; those were not bypassed.
- Public pages did not reliably disclose the split between official questions, staff-authored questions, licensed third-party questions and AI-generated questions.
- No revenue, subscriber or conversion figures were found from primary public sources.

## Commentary, cases, and transparency decisions on exam-question copyright and LPI article 13

### Takeaway

The sources reveal a live distinction, not a settled answer: article 13 LPI excludes specified official texts, but does not expressly name exam questions; public bodies and transparency authorities have taken opposing positions on whether tribunal-created question sets are protected. Transparency decisions strongly support access to completed exams, yet access is not the same as a commercial reuse licence. No directly on-point final Spanish appellate ruling deciding that tribunal-written exam questions do—or do not—fall within article 13 was located.

### Cited Findings

- **Statutory text:** article 13 LPI says exactly: “No son objeto de propiedad intelectual las disposiciones legales o reglamentarias y sus correspondientes proyectos, las resoluciones de los órganos jurisdiccionales y los actos, acuerdos, deliberaciones y dictámenes de los organismos públicos, así como las traducciones oficiales de todos los textos anteriores.” Exam questions are not expressly listed. Article 10 separately protects “creaciones originales literarias, artísticas o científicas expresadas por cualquier medio o soporte.” — [BOE, TRLPI](https://boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-1996-8930) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Reasoned institutional commentary, but not exam-specific precedent:** Universidad Politécnica de Madrid explains that article 13 texts may be reproduced/distributed/communicated/modified freely, while original studies or comments about them can be protected, and recommends using original official sources. It does not say whether a tribunal’s question paper is an “acto” or another listed category. — [UPM IP FAQ](https://www.upm.es/propiedad_intelectual/categoria/0/todas-las-preguntas/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Originality standard:** commentary summarising CJEU case law states that protection requires the author’s “creación intelectual propia,” reflected in free and creative choices; functionally constrained expression may fail that standard. This supports analysing each question’s expression and constraints, but does not itself classify Spanish public exams. — [Crowd Legal originality guide](https://crowd.legal/guias/obras-protegidas-y-originalidad) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Police/Guardia Civil position in transparency litigation:** in CTBG R/0691/2019, the administration argued that questionnaire creation “cristaliza en un conjunto de ítems” subject to IP because it results from tribunal members’ intellectual effort, initially belonging to authors and potentially transferring to the Police training division. This is an administration’s litigation position, not a judicial holding. — [CTBG Resolution 0691/2019](https://consejodetransparencia.es/content/dam/ctransparencia/portal-ctbg/actividad/resoluciones/resolucionesage/age2019/12diciembre/descomprimidas/R-0691-2019.pdf) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Contrary CTBG reasoning:** Resolution 273/2020 reproduces CTBG precedent stating that exam statements and correction templates are public information and, “con carácter general,” belong to the Administration rather than the officials who devised them or candidates; CTBG therefore considered the IP access limit inapplicable in that context. This is an access-law administrative interpretation, not a dispositive copyright infringement judgment. — [CTBG Resolution 273/2020](https://consejodetransparencia.es/content/dam/ctransparencia/portal-ctbg/actividad/resoluciones/resolucionesage/age-2020/07julio/descomprimidas/desestimadas/R-0273-2020.pdf) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **ADIF access dispute:** CTBG’s RCA277 record says ADIF denied three sets of exams under article 14.1(j) LTAIBG (professional secrecy/IP). CTBG required a concrete “test del daño”: a generic invocation is insufficient; the protected right must be specifically prejudiced, not merely affected. The record associates the dispute with Judgment 93/2023 of Juzgado Central de lo Contencioso-Administrativo nº 2 de Madrid. This supports access scrutiny, but the public summary does not decide commercial republication rights. — [CTBG RCA277 case page](https://consejodetransparencia.es/reclamaciones/recursos_jurisprudencia/recursos-ambito-estatal/2023/recursosministerios/277-mtransportes) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Guardia Civil and Police examination access:** CTBG Resolutions 016/022-2019 addressed requests for statements, answer sheets and correction materials from Guardia Civil/Police processes and treated statements/correction criteria as public-information access issues. Again, disclosure does not by itself grant downstream commercial reuse. — [CTBG Resolutions 016/022-2019](https://consejodetransparencia.es/content/dam/ctransparencia/portal-ctbg/actividad/resoluciones/resolucionesage/age2019/03marzo/DESCOMPRIMIDAS/estimadas/R-0016%20y%20R-0022-2019.pdf) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Article 14 LTAIBG separates access from IP merits.** The transparency limit applies where access would prejudice “el secreto profesional y la propiedad intelectual e industrial,” with a justified and proportionate case-specific analysis. A successful access request therefore does not prove the material is outside copyright or reusable commercially. — [BOE, Ley 19/2013](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2013-12887) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)
- **Commercial commentary should not be treated as authority.** Red Opositor publicly asserts that official exam questions are public domain under article 13, while other prep commentary says reproduction may be illegal because the organ owns the questionnaire. The contradiction itself shows why neither proposition should be adopted without primary authority. — [Red Opositor market commentary](https://redopositor.com/blog/es-comparativa-plataformas-test-oposiciones-2026/) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`); [Opositor.com commentary](https://www.opositor.com/puedo-llevarme-copia-preguntas-examenes-oposiciones/36881) (`opened_on: 2026-11-17`)

### Inferences

- There are at least three distinct questions that should not be conflated:
  1. whether the public can **access** an exam under transparency law;
  2. whether a question’s wording meets the **originality** threshold;
  3. whether a tribunal paper is an article 13 “acto, acuerdo, deliberación o dictamen,” or instead a protected work whose reuse is licensed by the relevant administration.
- A transparency authority can order disclosure while copyright/reuse restrictions remain; conversely, a portal licence may authorise reuse without resolving article 13 status.
- For product design, the uncertainty favours an evidence-based licence allowlist and independently authored questions from BOE statutory text, while retaining official restricted questions as metadata only.

### Gaps

- No CENDOJ/BOE-published final appellate decision was found squarely deciding the article 13 status of tribunal-written opposition questions.
- Judgment 93/2023 is identified by CTBG, but the retrieved public case page did not expose a full judicial analysis of article 13 LPI or downstream reuse rights.
- No authoritative source was found resolving authorship where questions are written by tribunal members in official duties versus external contractors, or whether a full question bank may receive database protection even if individual questions do not.
