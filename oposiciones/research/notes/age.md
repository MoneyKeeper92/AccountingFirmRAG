Every PDF URL listed below was opened directly on 2026-11-17 and returned HTTP 200 unless expressly noted. “Text PDF” means selectable text was successfully extracted; “scan” means extraction produced no usable text. ## Which official papers and final keys exist by year/access/model?

### Takeaway
The current Sede archive exposes complete questionnaire/provisional-key/final-key sets for the 2019, 2020, 2022, 2024 and 2025 convocatorias (the exam may occur in a later calendar year). Model A/B begins in the 2024 and 2025 cycles; earlier ordinary sittings normally have one model, with separate extraordinary sittings where applicable. The legacy INAP page also lists older exercises back to 2010, but those legacy groups are chiefly promotion-interna or second-exercise papers and do not consistently expose answer keys.

- Relevant quoted conditions: “Está prohibido desnaturalizar el sentido de la información”; “Debe citarse la fuente… ‘Origen de los datos: Instituto Nacional de Administración Pública’”; the last-update date must be stated when present; reuse must not imply INAP sponsorship; and update/reuse metadata must be preserved. — [INAP legal notice](https://www.inap.es/es/aviso-legal)
- The notice says use is at the reuser’s own risk, INAP disclaims responsibility for reuse and does not guarantee continuity, form, or error-free availability. — [INAP legal notice](https://www.inap.es/es/aviso-legal)
- The portal IP clause states: “Se prohíbe la reproducción total o parcial de los contenidos del Portal sin citar su origen o solicitar autorización.” — [INAP legal notice](https://www.inap.es/es/aviso-legal)
- INAP’s open-data page says it uses the basic availability mode under art. 8.1 RD 1495/2011 and repeats the five conditions: preserve meaning, cite source, state update date, do not imply sponsorship, preserve metadata. — [INAP open-data page](https://www.inap.es/es/datos-abiertos)

### Inferences
- Safe reuse practice for derived practice material: cite “Origen de los datos: Instituto Nacional de Administración Pública,” retain source URL and original update/publication date, label adaptations, avoid implying endorsement, and preserve available metadata.
- Broad portal wording supports reuse, but “documentos sometidos a ellas” leaves enough scope ambiguity that verbatim full-question republication should be cleared with INAP or tied to an explicit dataset/licence designation.
