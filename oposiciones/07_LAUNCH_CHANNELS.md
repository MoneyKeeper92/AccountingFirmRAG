## 1. Community rules we follow (non-negotiable)

1. **Always disclose**: "Lo he hecho yo / somos el equipo de Tesón". No fake "I found this
   great app" posts. Opositores spot astroturfing fast, and it gets you banned and remembered.
2. **Ask admins/mods before posting**, and offer them something (a pinned weekly free
   simulacro, a branded test for their group, early access).
3. **Give value first**: daily questions, explained exams, calculators. The link goes under
   the value.
4. **No cross-posting spam**: one tailored post per community, spaced out.
5. **No email walls** on free content. The calculators, article pages and explained exams are
   open. Sign-up only saves progress.
6. **Stay out of competitor-owned spaces** (OpositaTest's Telegram/Facebook, MAD's Mundo Opositor).
7. **Real humans post.** Claude drafts; a human (Kyle, or the community helper below) posts
   from a genuine account. Automated posting breaks platform rules.

**Who posts?** If Kyle doesn't want to (or his Spanish isn't native-level), recruit a
**community helper**: a current opositor from the beta, paid ~€200–400/month for ~5 h/week, or a
free "Fundador+" perk. They post, answer comments, and run 2–4 interviews a month. See the open
questions in [10](10_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

## 5. Referral mechanics (built in, low effort)
- "Invita a tu compañero de estudio": both get a "Fundador+" badge and early access to new
  features. **No money**, which keeps it simple legally.
- Shareable result card after each simulacro ("He sacado 61,3 en mi simulacro de Tesón"),
  made for WhatsApp and Instagram stories and carrying a UTM link.
- Study-group feature later (month 3+) if interviews ask for it.
