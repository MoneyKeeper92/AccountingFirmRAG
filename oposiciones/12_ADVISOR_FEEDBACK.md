# 12 — Questions for Kyle's Spanish advisors + the feedback app

**App ("Consulta Tesón"):** https://claude.ai/artifact/M5SPpFMpDYjy4qP4bVbvwy (source:
[tools/consulta-teson.html](tools/consulta-teson.html))

## How to use it

1. Open the link and use the **Share** menu to create a link anyone can open. The artifact
   is private until you do this.
2. Send the link to each advisor by WhatsApp. Suggested message:
   > ¡Hola! Estoy preparando un proyecto nuevo para opositores y me vendría genial tu opinión.
   > Son unos 10 minutos y todo es opcional: <link>. ¡Gracias!
3. They answer on their phone. Answers are saved in their browser as they go, so they can
   close the page and come back later.
4. At the end they tap **Enviar por WhatsApp** (or copy/paste). Each reply arrives as
   plain text with question codes like `[B3]`.
5. Paste all the replies into a Claude chat and ask for a synthesis. The codes make them
   easy to tally, and then we update the plan.

**Why WhatsApp and not a database:** a page that stores answers on the server can only be
used by signed-in members of your Claude organization. Your advisors don't have that, so
this version asks nothing of them beyond opening a link.

## Who to send it to
- **Anyone Spanish:** Parts 1–4 (name, product, channels). Their gut reaction to the name
  and tone matters most.
- **People who have opposed or are funcionarios:** everything. They are also your best
  candidates for reviewer and helper.
- **A gestor, lawyer or accountant friend:** Part 5 in particular.

Aim for **8–15 responses** before the Chrome session. That's enough to confirm or kill the name
and adjust the tagline and channels.
